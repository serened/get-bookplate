## A clean home library spreadsheet

Free template for Google Sheets and Excel. No signup, no watermark, no clutter.

### What's inside

- One row per book: ISBN, title, author, genre, format, publisher, year, pages
- A place for what makes each copy yours: where it lives, and when it joined your shelves
- A reading section for your journal: status, rating, when you read it, what you thought
- Dropdowns for genre, format, location, and status, fed by lists you control
- Four example rows to show the idea

### How to use it

1. Open it in Google Sheets (File, then Make a copy) or Excel.
2. Add one row per book. The ISBN is on the back cover or the copyright page.
3. Tune the Lists tab so the dropdowns match your shelves.
4. Sort and filter freely. The header row stays put.

### When the spreadsheet starts to feel like homework

Typing every title is fine for fifty books. It stops being fine around a few
hundred. Bookplate reads this exact file: import it and your covers, page
counts, and publication details fill in on their own, with your genres and
shelf locations intact. Your reading columns come too. Statuses, ratings,
dates, and notes land in your private reading journal, not just a list of
what you own.
